Physics of Sound

Understanding Waveforms & Harmonics

Sine waves represent pure fundamental tones without additional harmonics, while square and sawtooth waves introduce odd and even harmonic series that define distinct sound timbres in synthesis.

Audio Engineering

The Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem

To accurately capture an analog audio signal digitally without aliasing distortion, the digital sample rate must be at least twice the highest audible frequency (e.g., 44.1 kHz sample rate for 20 kHz human hearing limit).
